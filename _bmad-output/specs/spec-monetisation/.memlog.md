- (note) CORRECTION — the box drop table DOES exist in code and I had not looked. functions/index.js:3094 generateGiftBoxReward. 95% of boxes pay 500-2000 nolics. The remaining 5% splits 95/5 into a mid tier (nolics 7000-13000, OR 1 gold, OR 1 day premium) and a rare tier (logo, trophy-or-10-20-gold, 10-20 gold, 30 days premium, or 50000-200000 nolics). Expected gold per box works out to roughly 0.019, i.e. about one gold per 53 boxes — which is exactly the owner's 'very little', now with a number.
- (note) Box accrual is also in code: advanceGiftBoxState + GIFT_BOX_STREAK_TARGET_DAYS=10. A daily visit increments a streak; from the tenth consecutive day it grants one box per day. With the five-ads box that caps the honest maximum at two boxes per day, which is the anti-cheat bound the owner described.
- (note) Premium already drops from boxes: 86400s (1 day) in the mid tier, 86400*30 (30 days) in the rare tier. So 'premium can drop from a box' is shipped behaviour, not new.
- (note) Referral in legacy: ReferalRemote(name, icon, allOpenBox, openBoxInSeason) with the author's own comment that a server function walks referrals and credits the referrer per boxes opened per season. So referral pays out on referred players' box openings, seasonally — amounts undefined.
- (direction) ANSWER — commission is 5%, paid by the BUYER.
- (direction) ANSWER — premium comes in four durations: day, week, month, year. Three acquisition routes: real money (credited immediately), a box drop, and Google Play. Benefits: golden nickname everywhere leaderboards appear and in chat (the chat one is the prized effect), box multipliers (x2, or several boxes per five ads, with a x5 beta bonus), possibly golden chat text, possibly faster charge regeneration, and sponsor-qualification points toward perks not yet defined.
- (constraint) OWNER'S DESIGN RULE for premium: it must never buy competitive advantage. Either the perk is personal and incomparable with other players, or, if it is comparable (a nickname colour), it carries no material advantage. This rules out premium affecting scores, income or ranking.
- (direction) ANSWER — leaderboard pays once per season, and a season is three months. Chosen over monthly for a bigger push and time to spend. Top 100: ranks 50-100 get 1 gold, 30-50 get 3, 10-30 get 5, 5-10 get 10, 4-5 get 14, third 25, second 50, first 100 plus a trophy. Owner is considering paying in BOXES instead of gold as the more interesting option.
- (direction) ANSWER — gifts are not a user-facing feature. They are manual grants by the server or the developer for things like a reported bug. Custom, not a mechanic.
- (direction) ANSWER — the only honest earning loop is: watch ads daily, keep the streak, and from day ten receive a box a day; five ads buy another. Two boxes a day is the ceiling, and a farming script is bounded by that ceiling and by the first ban.
- (direction) ANSWER — the special-ads screen is a separate screen on the home tab, provisionally called Offers. Users buy their own ad placement with GOLD through an auction: submit an image and a link, bid gold, watch an open table, and the top bid takes the slot for the period (a month, possibly a week). Tapping the screen shows the winning offer's image, link, and a button to enter the auction.
