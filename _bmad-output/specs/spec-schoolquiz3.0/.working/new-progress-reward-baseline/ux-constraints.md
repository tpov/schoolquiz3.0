# Screen experience constraints

These are the accepted visual and interaction constraints at the imported UX cutoff: world-r2's targeted CTA change, plus the owner's September 30 screen-content instruction. They constrain further work; they do not mean that every screen, all of its content or the overall UX is approved.

## Separate content confirmation

**Every screen's content must be confirmed separately against the overall product logic, even if its appearance is approved.** Existing explicit decisions remain constraints for this review. Demo copy, values, actions and missing states cannot be treated as approved production content merely because a visual was accepted.

## Retained constraints

- **UX-1:** Keep Home, runner and Shop as the visual baseline; preserve approved screens except for explicitly accepted later changes. “Minimalism” does not authorize a replacement flat style.

- **UX-2:** Keep the main profile on one shareable/screenshot screen: nickname, main percentage, radar with both qualification/role-activity series, and separate trophy tiles. Include rating if it fits; do not replace the radar with a table or accordion. Eight trophies and a 390×844 preview are fixtures, not global data/device limits.

- **UX-3:** Use app chrome #08080A for top/status/bottom/side navigation surfaces across all screens, including earlier approved views. Do not darken the content/cards merely because the chrome is dark.

- **UX-4:** Preserve the original Home composition and Continue with its accepted 5% white fill. Shop balances sit to the right of the title; nolics are gray and gold is gold, also in prices. Restore runner single from actual accepted source/application behavior, not the rejected HTML variant.

- **UX-5:** Keep the accepted lesson list without added Start/Open buttons; when an exam exists, its gold card is at the bottom. Do not invent price or admission. Archive uses the same card family with Download or Downloaded/Check at the right.

- **UX-6:** My Quests has category selection and a + FAB that creates a category. Before approval, show review strips and a short status; after approval, replace the strips with a horizontal row of publication-place tags, with details expandable. Review strips are not player progress, a new role count or additional stage fees; bind them to permitted revision/publication projections.

- **UX-7:** The confirmed-result composition keeps percent, personal best, one reward strip, rating, top, discussion and text actions. Keep the two direct Comments/Report error actions and typed pending/unknown/recovery states. Approval of result-r1 is a Compose-derived visual reference, not a claim of pixel equality or removal of unshown states.

- **UX-8:** NEXT selects the next lesson by order within the current theme; if none exists, EXAM navigates to the applicable assessment/admission surface. AGAIN repeats; labels match the action. Discussion shows total comments, no new/unread marker, and opens a modal list. EXAM never bypasses eligibility, sync, bank readiness, price consent or explicit paid start, and section completion cannot issue a certificate.

- **UX-9:** Arena keeps the purple background, the accepted lesson-card family and a magnifier FAB for random search. The preview's three-card selection is not the production algorithm; server RANDOM preparation, reserves and recovery remain binding.

- **UX-10:** Qualification participants show nickname/avatar, status, attempt count, last result and last-attempt time, with All/With attempts/Without attempts filters. Do not substitute a personal best or place for the last result, or interpret no attempts as confirmed 0%. Preview people/counts/values are fixtures.

- **UX-11:** Qualification ranking uses compact result-style rows, a gold leader and highlighted self/result. Average result displays averagePercent, not ratingPercent; client display never reorders authoritative place/order by that average. Qualification cutoff/status comes from the event rule: current MVP top16 per FR-36. Top32 in the approved visual preview does not amend the product rule or assert qualification.

- **UX-12:** Preserve the accepted appearance of runner_single, profile-v2-new, profile-rename and nft-logos without inferring new capabilities. Approval of an NFT-logo visual does not import the old market/referral scope.

- **UX-13:** The World CTA uses the approved Home Continue structure with green success #5CC97A; do not invent completion counts or progress for a tournament that has not started. Preserve the three menu cards, purple background and dark chrome. This targeted world-r2 instruction is accepted; the World screen as a whole is not yet approved.

- **UX-14:** Every screen's content needs separate explicit owner confirmation against the overall product logic, even when its appearance is already approved. Review texts, displayed data, action behavior and relevant states; an accepted visual preview is not blanket content approval. Preserve existing explicit product decisions as constraints and do not treat fixtures or unreviewed copy as final. The accepted subset above guides this review but does not declare all screen contents approved.
