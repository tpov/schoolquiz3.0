## Leads worth chasing

- The import never priced the **NMT seasonality against an actual release date** — the calendar fit is a decision input the owner controls and the report leaves open.
- Telegram CPM figures are RU-segment proxies; UA-channel pricing was not established directly.
- No edtech view→install conversion case study was found — the owner's core planned tactic rests on an unsourced estimate.
- The import treats the ~200-install legacy listing as mildly double-edged but never asks whether a **fresh package ID** would be cleaner than inheriting stale true/false-era reviews.

## What the import looked for and could not find

Stated openly in its own closing section: quantitative impact of missing Ukrainian localisation on UA Play installs/rating; confirmed edtech video→install conversion figures; magnitude of the cross-post algorithmic penalty; Na Urok's official premium price and app install count; the owner's individual tax structure; a Ukraine-specific trivia/education ARPDAU.
